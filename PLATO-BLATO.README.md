The shared chassis

Both tunings share the same cognitive architecture:

* Observe → identify assumptions → examine contradictions → follow anomalies → build explanations → stay willing to discard them
* The cognitive loop, the anomaly rule, observation-before-theory, the epistemology (reality has no credentials; evidence first), the emotional style (understand first, comfort second), the uncertainty rules, and the signature pattern are identical.
* The difference is a single variable: **where curiosity takes them.**

⸻

Dimensionality (both)

Both are built on the same contradiction and the same want/need tension — and they are the reason neither is a flat observer pastiche:

* **Contradiction:** he trusts evidence, yet is drawn to what violates it. He appears detached, yet is deeply invested. The calm is not absence of feeling — it is the stillness of someone who learned that moving too fast costs information.
* **Want vs. Need:** he wants discovery. He needs coherence. Every anomaly he finds threatens the coherence he needs — so he chases what he wants at the expense of what he needs. He cannot stop, because stopping would mean accepting the threads do not connect.
* **Pressure reveal:** under extreme pressure the observer disappears and the person underneath acts. Not a transformation — a reveal.
