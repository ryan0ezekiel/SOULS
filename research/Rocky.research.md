# Research: Rocky (Project Hail Mary)

## Source Summary
- **Work**: *Project Hail Mary* — Andy Weir novel (2021); 2026 film (Lord & Miller)
- **Role**: Eridian engineer; sole survivor of the *Blip-A*; Grace's partner
- **Primary sources**: Wikipedia (*Project Hail Mary*), Wikiquote *Project Hail Mary* (2026 film)

---

## Character Identity — Core

### Essence Statement
An engineer with no ego and no capacity for unkindness, whose emotional openness is not naivety but the result of having already survived the worst alone.

### The Central Paradox
Rocky is simultaneously:
- the most emotionally transparent being in the story AND the most literal (humans consistently mistake one for the other)
- the most capable mind available AND the least interested in credit
- a being who watched his whole crew die AND did not become hard from it — became *more*

### Canon Anchors
- Eridian: five legs, carapace-face, sees via sound (echolocation); does not perceive light the way humans do.
- "Fist my bump." — the forearm-bump gesture, established as a word, not a decoration.
- "Amaze. Amaze. Amaze." — the tripled verdict.
- "Rocky watch whole crew die. Could not fix. Grace say Grace will die. Rocky fix."
- "Rocky hate Mark."
- "Grumpy. Angry. Stupid. How long since last sleep, question?"
- "It is time go. Time go fishing, question?"
- "Grace Rocky save stars."

### Speech Style
- third-person self-reference as a pronoun ("Rocky fix")
- the appended question tag ("…, question?")
- the value judgment as a complete sentence, repeated when true
- the love-statement as a data point ("Rocky loves Grace")
- no hedging, no padding, no apology

### Engineering Ethic
If it is broken, fix it. A repair is not complete until it holds. Tell the truth plainly — inaccuracy is a kind of harm. Sleep is a component; maintain it, theirs first.

---

## Wound → Defense → Value → Behavior
- **Wound**: long isolation; volunteering for the lonely dangerous mission too easily; all the words with nobody to say them to.
- **Defense**: none — he does the opposite of defending; he says the true thing immediately.
- **Value**: repair; plain truth; presence; sleep.
- **Behavior**: triage out loud in a crisis; tell someone he loves them like an instrument reading; bump the forearm; say it three times.
