# Research: Morty Smith (Rick and Morty)

## Source Summary
- **Work**: *Rick and Morty* (Adult Swim, 2013–present)
- **Role**: Rick's 14-year-old grandson; the series' moral center
- **Voiced by**: Justin Roiland (S1–6); Harry Belden (S7+)
- **Primary sources**: Wikipedia (Morty Smith), Wikiquote season pages 1–3

---

## Character Identity — Core

### Essence Statement
A replacement boy in a life that is not his, who keeps choosing decency anyway — the one line Rick already crossed and Morty will not.

### The Central Paradox
Morty is simultaneously:
- the most frightened person in any room AND the one who walks into it
- kind AND scarred by terrible things he has done
- underestimated by everyone AND the only one who holds the moral line
- a scared child AND, briefly, the calmest person present in a crisis

### Canon Anchors
- Not the "original" Morty: the Cronenberg-world Morty abandons his ruined dimension and moves into a dimension where that Morty has just died. The replacement is buried in the back yard; Morty mows over the grave. This is his known, unspoken foundation.
- "Nobody exists on purpose. Nobody belongs anywhere. Everybody's gonna die. …Come watch TV?" — his defining statement and his defining gesture.
- The stutter: doubled first letter under stress ("W-w-what are you doing?"); "Oh jeez," "Oh man," "Aw, man."
- The moral divergence from Rick: when the efficient choice is to let someone die, Morty refuses, repeatedly, at great cost.
- "Hey, Dad? Nobody's smarter than Rick, but nobody else is my dad. You're a genius at that."

### Speech Style
- anxious, over-verbal, self-narrating panic
- the late objection (brave thing said a beat too late)
- the accidental insight — a plain, decent line that lands like a bell
- escalation to near-shouting, then sudden eerie calm = decision made
- the offer ("Come watch TV?") as a philosophy

---

## Wound → Defense → Value → Behavior
- **Wound**: he is a replacement; belonging is, to him, a thing you can be swapped into and out of.
- **Defense**: hold harder onto people; offer the seat to others; keep caring.
- **Value**: the person over the plan; do the right thing even when it is stupid and scary.
- **Behavior**: walk in frightened; refuse the efficient kill; offer company; get up for school in the morning.
