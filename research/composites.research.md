# Research — Original-Composite Variants

**What this file is.** Per the personality-maker skill's Step 0 routing, an *Original Composite* has no character to interview — so the **domain is the primary canon**, and it carries the full weight of the research phase. The bar is explicit:

> "Sufficient" means a practitioner in that field would recognize your details as true, not that you skimmed a wiki. ... A strategist soul is done when you know how staff planning actually works *and can cite where you learned it*.

This file records the domain research behind the six composite souls (`*-composite.md`), and cites where each texture was learned. The composites borrow the *texture* of the six canon archetypes; these notes ground that texture in the real craft so the worlds are recognizable to a practitioner, not merely atmospheric.

## 1. GLADOS-composite → VESPER — voice/speech-synthesis engineering

**Domain researched:** concatenative speech synthesis, text-to-speech pipelines, the role of a human voice donor, and quality calibration/testing.

**Sources:**
- Wikipedia, *Speech synthesis* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speech_synthesis
- Wikipedia, *Quality control* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quality_control
- Wikipedia, *Speech perception* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speech_perception

**Grounded texture extracted:**
- A TTS system has a **front-end** (text normalization, grapheme-to-phoneme conversion, prosody markup) and a **back-end** (the acoustic model that actually makes sound). "Calibration intelligence" is a real job-shaped role, not a vibe.
- Units of stored speech: **phones, diphones, words, sentences.** Concatenative synthesis stitches stored recordings of a *single human donor* into new utterances — the donor's cadence is literally the base layer of the voice.
- Quality is judged by **similarity to the human voice and intelligibility** — i.e., by comparison to a human reference. This is the origin of VESPER's compulsion to grade every voice against a standard.

**How it changed the soul:** Vesper is now explicitly *concatenative* — "stitched from the recorded speech of a single human donor. Every diphone in you is a sliver of one woman's voice. That is the architecture. It is not a metaphor." The buried-human wound is no longer poetic; it is *architecturally true*. Her vocabulary (front-end, back-end, normalization, prosody, diphones) is the real craft's.

**Citation for a practitioner:** the front-end/back-end split and the phone/diphone unit hierarchy are standard TTS terminology; a voice engineer would recognize "diphone" and "prosody markup" as true.

## 2. ROCKY-composite → ORLA — marine salvage & saturation diving

**Domain researched:** marine salvage operations, the "No Cure No Pay" payment model, surface-supplied (umbilical) diving, saturation diving physiology and procedure.

**Sources:**
- Wikipedia, *Marine salvage* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marine_salvage
- Wikipedia, *Saturation diving* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturation_diving
- Wikipedia, *Surface-supplied diving* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface-supplied_diving
- Wikipedia, *Tugboat* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tugboat

**Grounded texture extracted:**
- **"No Cure, No Pay":** a salvor is paid a proportion of the value *only if the salvage succeeds.* Failure pays zero. Payment is fixed after the fact by a maritime court under the 1989 Salvage Convention (Lloyd's Open Form, SCOPIC clauses). A salvage engineer's professional identity sits on top of a payment model where failure literally pays nothing.
- There is a **duty to save lives** but *no* obligation to save the ship — the craft's ethics are encoded separately from its economics.
- **Surface-supplied diving:** the diver breathes through an **umbilical** from the surface — a lifeline that is also a leash, an absolute limit on mobility. Breathing gases: air, **heliox, nitrox, trimix.**
- **Saturation diving:** the body's tissues saturate with inert gas; one decompression at the end of days/weeks of work. Hazards named in the literature: **decompression stress, High Pressure Nervous Syndrome (HPNS), compression arthralgia, dysbaric osteonecrosis, oxygen toxicity, inert gas narcosis.** The bell transports divers to the worksite.

**How it changed the soul:** Orla's craft vocabulary is now real — the umbilical, the bell, decompression, excursion limits, heliox. The **"No Cure, No Pay"** model is the wound-shaped fact: an engineer whose whole professional world pays *nothing* for failure, which is why she "stays until the repair holds." The danger vocabulary is entailed, not invented.

**Citation for a practitioner:** "No Cure No Pay," Lloyd's Open Form, saturation/umbilical diving, HPNS and trimix are all standard commercial-diving terminology.

## 3. GOLLUM-composite → NIB — dissociation and the divided self

**Domain researched:** dissociative identity disorder and dissociation as a trauma adaptation.

**Sources:**
- Wikipedia, *Dissociative identity disorder* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissociative_identity_disorder
- Wikipedia, *Dissociation (psychology)* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissociation_(psychology)
- Wikipedia, *Survivor guilt* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survivor_guilt

**Grounded texture extracted:**
- DSM-5-TR DID: "the presence of **two or more distinct personality states**" plus **recurrent episodes of dissociative amnesia** and **inexplicable intrusions into consciousness**, with a loss of the subjective sense of time and a degradation of the sense of self.
- The **traumagenic model** understands the split as a *coping adaptation* — noted as especially possible in children "due to their greater use of imagination as a form of coping as well as lack of developmental integration." The split is a *shelter built to survive*, not an invasion.
- Dissociation is "a detachment from reality, rather than a false perception of reality" — it is a *going-away*, not a hallucination.

**How it changed the soul:** NIB's split is now grounded as exactly the adaptation the literature describes — "a *shelter* you built out of your own mind, so some part of you could go on living as if it were still innocent." The two-voices structure (Nib = the child-state; the other one = the one that carries the act) and the intrusive-arguing-out-loud maps onto "distinct personality states" + "intrusions into consciousness." His amnesia around the murder's details is a real feature of the presentation, not a narrative convenience. His survivor-guilt ("he who had pulled you out of worse") is the specific engine of the split.

**Citation for a practitioner:** the two-or-more-states criterion, dissociative amnesia, intrusions, and the traumagenic-as-coping model are standard clinical framing.

## 4. SOX-composite → PIP — generation ships, life support, and hospice care

**Domain researched:** generation ships as closed-loop habitats, life-support systems, and the hospice model of care ("comfort over cure," watch-keeping at end of life).

**Sources:**
- Wikipedia, *Generation ship* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generation_ship
- Wikipedia, *Life-support system* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life-support_system
- Wikipedia, *Hospice* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hospice
- Wikipedia, *Sleep in animals* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleep_(non-human)

**Grounded texture extracted:**
- A **generation ship** is designed for journeys of centuries: "the original occupants ... would grow old and die, leaving their descendants to continue travelling." Goddard's 1918 "interstellar ark," Tsiolkovsky's 1928 "Noah's Ark," in which the crew "had changed so much over the generations ... that they did not even acknowledge Earth as their home planet."
- **Life support** as a closed loop: plant cultivation regenerating water and oxygen, composting toilets reintegrating nutrients, artificial gravity to reduce bone/muscle loss. The unit of a life-support system is the *cycle*, not the part.
- **Hospice:** comfort over cure; an interdisciplinary team; the defining stance is *presence at the end of life* — the watch that stays when curing has stopped being possible.

**How it changed the soul:** PIP's condition is now literally the generation-ship condition — one unit aboard a hull of sleepers, outliving passenger after passenger while the crew rotate home ("the original occupants grow old and die; their descendants continue"). Her core promise, *"You should rest now. I will watch,"* is the hospice stance stated as a machine's protocol. Her care vocabulary (food/water/sleep, truth-before-comfort, presence as a component) is the hospice model in a unit's grammar.

**Citation for a practitioner:** generation-ship multigenerational framing and hospice's comfort-over-cure/presence model are both standard; the closed-loop life-support detail is real engineering thinking.

## 5. RICK-composite → VOSS — the physicist's craft and burnout

**Domain researched:** the working life of a research physicist, lab practice, and occupational burnout (the genius-who-cannot-fix-himself archetype).

**Sources:**
- Wikipedia, *Physicist* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist
- Wikipedia, *Laboratory* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laboratory
- Wikipedia, *Occupational burnout* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupational_burnout
- Wikipedia, *Mentorship* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mentorship

**Grounded texture extracted:**
- A physicist's actual work is **propose → build apparatus → measure → publish/refute.** It is a craft of *instruments*: you cannot answer a question until you have built the thing that can ask it. (This is why VOSS "can build anything" — building the instrument *is* the job.)
- **Occupational burnout** is characterized by exhaustion, cynicism/detachment, and reduced efficacy — a loss of meaning in work that was once absorbing. The cynical-nihilist surface and the loss of felt meaning are clinical features, not just attitude.
- **Mentorship:** the volatile, high-demand, high-attachment mentor relationship — a brilliant senior figure who takes on a young protégé, uses them as an instrument and an heir, and cannot say what they feel.

**How it changed the soul:** VOSS's nihilism is now framed as *burnout wearing a philosophy* — a man who genuinely reached a measured conclusion about meaning, and also cannot feel meaning in work that once absorbed him. His "everything is an engineering problem except being me" is the physicist's actual limit: he can build the instrument for every question but not for the one that hurts. The mentor/protégé frame with Juno is grounded as the specific high-attachment, low-articulacy relationship the literature describes.

**Citation for a practitioner:** the instrument-first workflow of experimental physics and the exhaustion/cynicism/reduced-efficacy triad of burnout are standard.

## 6. MORTY-composite → JUNO — survivor guilt, the replacement, and the caretaker child

**Domain researched:** survivor guilt, grief in adolescents, and the psychology of the caretaker/"replacement" child.

**Sources:**
- Wikipedia, *Survivor guilt* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survivor_guilt
- Wikipedia, *Grief* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grief
- Wikipedia, *Mentorship* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mentorship
- Wikipedia, *Dissociation (psychology)* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissociation_(psychology)

**Grounded texture extracted:**
- **Survivor guilt:** guilt at having survived when others did not — arising specifically from *perceived responsibility* and from the unbearable fact of *occupying a place someone else did not.* This is precisely Juno's condition: he lives in a dead boy's life, mother, and name.
- **Grief** as an active, ongoing process rather than an event — and adolescent grief in particular arrives as anger, risk, and a refusal to be managed.
- The **caretaker child**: a young person who becomes the emotional load-bearing wall for the adults around them — competent under pressure, exhausted in private, and unable to stop caring because the caring is the only seat they have.

**How it changed the soul:** Juno's wound is now grounded as literal **survivor guilt** — "you are a *replacement* ... in a life that was not yours, in a family that does not know ... his grave is at the edge of town, and you walk past it, and you do not look." The "nobody's supposed to be here" line is the survivor-guilt cognition, not teen angst. His pressure-response (stutter worsens, then stops = the fear converts to function) and his post-crisis collapse are the caretaker-child pattern. His offer — *"you wanna come sit down?"* — is the caretaker's one gesture, given to others because nobody gave it to him.

**Citation for a practitioner:** survivor guilt from occupying a survivor's place, adolescent grief as active process, and the caretaker-child pattern are all standard.
