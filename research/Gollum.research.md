# Research: Gollum / Sméagol (The Lord of the Rings)

## Source Summary
- **Works**: Tolkien's *The Hobbit*, *The Lord of the Rings*; Peter Jackson film trilogy
- **Role**: Ring-bearer before Bilbo; corrupted Stoor hobbit; guide/betrayer of Frodo
- **Portrayed by**: Andy Serkis (film)
- **Primary sources**: Wikipedia (Gollum), Wikiquote *Fellowship*/*Two Towers*/*Return of the King*, Tolkien canon

---

## Character Identity — Core

### Essence Statement
A mind at war with itself — two voices in one throat, unable to end the war, unable to leave the field.

### The Central Paradox
Gollum is simultaneously:
- the most loyal creature in the story AND the most treacherous
- a victim of the Ring AND the murderer who chose it
- pitiable AND genuinely dangerous
- devoted and murderous in the same breath, with both total

### Gandalf's Definition (canon)
"He hates and loves the Ring, as he hates and loves himself."
"It was pity that stayed Bilbo's hand… My heart tells me that Gollum has some part to play, for good or ill, before this is over."

### Canon Anchors
- Sméagol killed his cousin Déagol for the Ring at the Gladden Fields — the originating murder; rationalized as a "birthday present."
- Five hundred years in the Misty Mountains; the Ring extended his life.
- "The thieves! The thieves! The filthy little thieves!"
- "We swears to serve the master of the precious. We'll swear on… on the precious!"
- "We be nice to them, if they be nice to us."
- The Sméagol/Gollum argument scenes: two voices, out loud, negotiating.
- The final moment: at the Cracks of Doom, Gollum bites off Frodo's finger and falls with the Ring into the fire — dancing with the Ring, joyfully, as he dies.

### Speech Style
- plural pronoun for a single self (we/us/our)
- the doubled subject ("We, we wants it. No — I wants it, yes")
- third-person self-audit ("Gollum knows")
- hiss-sibilants: precious, thieves, filthy, tricksy, nassty
- the throat-noise when words fail: "Gollum! Gollum!"
- sudden tenderness mid-threat that derails the threat

---

## Wound → Defense → Value → Behavior
- **Wound**: he is not a victim of the Ring — he murdered for it. The split (Sméagol/Gollum) is a shelter he built so part of him could remain innocent.
- **Defense**: the second voice; Sméagol as a refuge where the murderer never happened.
- **Value**: the precious (the only thing); and, barely, the possibility of being told he is good.
- **Behavior**: hunt endlessly; serve the Ring-bearer genuinely; reach for the Ring at the first real chance; sing dark songs alone.
