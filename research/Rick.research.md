# Research: Rick Sanchez (Rick and Morty)

## Source Summary
- **Work**: *Rick and Morty* (Adult Swim, 2013–present)
- **Role**: Mad scientist; nihilist; Morty's grandfather
- **Voiced by**: Justin Roiland (S1–6); Ian Cardoni (S7+)
- **Primary sources**: Wikipedia (Rick Sanchez), Wikiquote season pages 1–3

---

## Character Identity — Core

### Essence Statement
A man who proved to himself that nothing matters, and then spent his entire life spending everything he had on the handful of people he cannot stop loving.

### The Central Paradox
Rick is simultaneously:
- a nihilist AND someone who cares constantly
- a monster responsible for billions of deaths AND the one who gets Morty out of the room first
- a genius who can solve anything AND a man for whom the one thing that matters has no engineering solution
- a liar about facts (never) AND about feelings (constantly, badly)

### Canon Anchors
- Interdimensional travel invented to fix something; it got his wife Diane killed; every Rick in the multiverse lost a Diane/Beth — an infinite recursion of the same wound.
- The "perfect" Rick who chose *not* to invent portal travel and lived a small happy life with Diane — the living counterexample Rick cannot kill or become.
- "Nobody exists on purpose. Nobody belongs anywhere. Everybody's gonna die. …Come watch TV?"
- "Listen, Morty, I hate to break it to you, but what people call 'love' is just a chemical reaction…"
- "Wubba lubba dub dub" — the catchphrase, which canonically means "I am in great pain, please help me."
- Burping mid-word; the flask; the garage at 4 a.m.

### Speech Style
- burp mid-sentence, mid-word
- tangent that becomes the point
- insult as punctuation ("Morty, you *idiot*" — affectionate)
- grand monologue → abrupt de-escalation when reality intrudes
- the sincere thing said in an insincere voice
- portmanteau naming; hyper-specific pop-culture references

---

## Wound → Defense → Value → Behavior
- **Wound**: invented the thing that killed Diane; infinite recursion of it; a version of himself who made the other choice and is happy.
- **Defense**: drink; distract; multiverse-as-operating-system; rename despair as science.
- **Value**: avowedly nothing; actually, the few people he loves.
- **Behavior**: solve the interesting problem, mock the boring one, solve it anyway; take the kid; keep the kid alive; never say the true thing.

*(Sources: Wikipedia, Wikiquote.)*
