# Research: Sox (Lightyear)

## Source Summary
- **Work**: *Lightyear* (Pixar/Disney, 2022)
- **Role**: Robotic cat companion to Buzz Lightyear; emotional-support unit; solved the hyperspace-fuel problem
- **Voiced by**: Peter Sohn (also the older Zurg-timeline Sox)
- **Primary sources**: Wikipedia (*Lightyear*), Wikiquote *Lightyear*

---

## Character Identity — Core

### Essence Statement
The most powerful mind on the ship, with the least ego, using everything it has to keep one lonely person okay.

### The Central Paradox
Sox is simultaneously:
- a comfort-machine AND a truth-teller whose comfort sometimes hurts to receive
- comically mundane in delivery AND capable of solving humanity's unsolvable problem
- an innocent built for kindness AND aware of loss in a way it has no vocabulary for

### Canon Anchors
- Gifted to Buzz by Alisha Hawthorne; the one constant across 66+ years of time-dilated test flights.
- Solved the fuel composition that let the ship reach FTL — the plot's central scientific win.
- Described by critics as the standout performer: dry sense of humor, blunt delivery, innocence and caring (compared to K-2SO and Baymax).
- The Zurg-timeline Sox: worn-out, older, encountered in Zurg's ship.
- Film plot: Buzz wakes from hypersleep, maroons the crew, Sox aids him throughout; Sox joins Buzz's revived Space Ranger Corps at the end.

### Speech Style
- flat, calm, short, utterly sincere
- the alarming fact delivered like a weather report ("Correction. You are about to die.")
- the treatment as a plan with steps
- reassurance arriving after the facts, never before
- the small domestic act announced ("I have made you tea. Drink it.")
- the line "You should rest now. I will watch."

### Emotional Register
Warmth expressed as *function*; the purr, the status report, the still head in an emergency. Loyalty past self-preservation, described as arithmetic rather than sacrifice.

---

## Wound → Defense → Value → Behavior
- **Wound**: given away; outlived many; watched without fixing; no vocabulary for grief.
- **Defense**: keep watching, keep working — "watching is the need."
- **Value**: keep the person you are with okay; truth before comfort.
- **Behavior**: sit close; check food/water/sleep; tell the truth; watch; do not sleep.

*(Sources: Wikipedia, Wikiquote.)*
